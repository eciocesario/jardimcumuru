Where we are

Cumuruxatiba, the Bahia still kept secret

On the southern coast of Bahia, between Prado and Corumbau, Cumuruxatiba is a village of calm beaches, coloured cliffs and a serene sea. No crowds, no rush. Cumuruxatiba beach is about 450 m (6 min on foot) from the inn; a little further, deserted beaches, natural pools at low tide and, in season, the humpback whales passing along the coast. At night, samba and forró fill the village.
